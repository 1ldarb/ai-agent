# TECHSTORE US KNOWLEDGE BASE - OFFICIAL CUSTOMER FAQ

## SECTION 1: SHIPPING & LOGISTICS (US NATIONWIDE)
Q: Which US cities do you ship to?
A: We provide fast shipping to all 50 states. Major hubs include New York City (NYC), Los Angeles (LA), Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Philadelphia, and San Antonio.

Q: What are the shipping rates for the Continental US?
A: Standard ground shipping (3-7 business days) is FREE for orders over $50. For orders under $50, a flat rate of $9.99 applies. 

Q: Do you offer expedited shipping to the West Coast?
A: Yes, we offer Next-Day Air and 2-Day Priority shipping via FedEx and UPS to California, Oregon, and Washington state for an additional fee calculated at checkout.

Q: Do you ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or Puerto Rico?
A: Yes, though shipping times may vary (usually 7-14 business days). Please note that free shipping promotions may not apply to these regions.

## SECTION 2: PAYMENTS, TAXES & FINANCING
Q: What payment methods are accepted in the US?
A: We accept all major US credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), as well as digital wallets like Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.

Q: Can I pay using Venmo or Cash App?
A: Yes, we support direct payments via Venmo and Cash App for mobile checkout users.

Q: How is Sales Tax calculated?
A: Sales tax is automatically calculated based on your shipping address and state regulations (e.g., NY, CA, TX). Taxes will be displayed at the final step of checkout.

Q: Do you offer installment plans (Buy Now, Pay Later)?
A: Yes, we partner with Affirm and Klarna to offer interest-free monthly installments for purchases over $500.

## SECTION 3: RETURNS & WARRANTY
Q: What is the US return policy?
A: We offer a 30-day "No Questions Asked" return policy for all electronics. Items must be in original packaging. A 15% restocking fee may apply to opened high-end laptops.

Q: How do I handle a warranty claim for a MacBook?
A: All MacBooks come with a 1-year Apple limited warranty. You can take your device to any authorized Apple Store in cities like Miami, Boston, or Seattle, or mail it to our service center.
